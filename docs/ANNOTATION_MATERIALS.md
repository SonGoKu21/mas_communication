# Taxonomy construction protocol

The final manuscript reports 31 source documents, 57 fault/effect descriptions, 43 candidate concepts and a 10-candidate pilot. Two researchers independently code source layer, receiver-visible manifestation and downstream MAS consequence; reported pre-adjudication Cohen’s kappa spans 0.79–0.86. A third researcher adjudicates disagreements to obtain 29 final categories.

`web/content/taxonomy.json` supplies the final category table, and `web/content/references.json` supplies the 30 bibliographic entries cited in the website taxonomy supplement. This reference list is not a 31-document construction-source inventory.

The released files do not contain the candidate-level independent labels, pilot membership, adjudication decisions or dimension-specific kappa calculation. The reported agreement is a manuscript claim, not independently recomputed by the released scripts. These records cannot be reconstructed from the final category table.
